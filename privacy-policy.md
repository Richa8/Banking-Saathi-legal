1. Introduction

Banking Saathi (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a conversational assistant designed to help users understand basic banking information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with the Banking Saathi bot on WhatsApp.

By using Banking Saathi, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you interact with the bot:

a. User Identification

Phone number (stored as user_id)
(If required in future, we may hash or anonymize this field.)

b. Message Data

Message type (text, voice, image, document)

Voice message URL (from WhatsApp Cloud API)

Voice message transcription

User query text (either direct text or generated transcription)

Media ID (for WhatsApp media)

Language detected (if and when implemented)

c. Bot Response Data

Gemini response text (AI-generated reply)

Intent classification (AI-understood intent of the message)

d. System Data

Timestamps: created_at and updated_at

Feedback state (if users provide feedback)

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

To operate and improve the Banking Saathi bot

To analyse queries and measure bot performance

To refine the accuracy of responses

To build metrics, analytics dashboards, and internal reports

To diagnose technical issues and improve reliability

Your data helps make the bot more useful for low-literacy users who need banking support.

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or share your personal information with any third party.
Your data is used only for internal bot improvement, analytics, and performance measurement.

We do not share your data with:

Advertisers

External businesses

Data brokers

Marketing partners

Data may be processed on:

WhatsApp Cloud API

Internal servers/databases

AI model providers (e.g., Gemini), only to generate bot responses

No third party receives your data for their own use.
